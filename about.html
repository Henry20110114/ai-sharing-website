平台介绍About the platform

AI无界平台是一个高中生视角的、非盈利性的、用于分享 AI 使用经验的平台。我们提供课程、答疑、学科和 AI 资讯，并组织研讨活动、工作坊、报告会等活动进行深度探索与交流，立志让高中生们取长补短，共同培养坚实的 AI 实践技能。TeenLens AI (AI无界) is a non-profit platform for sharing experience with AI, built from a high-school student's perspective. We offer courses, Q&A, subject guides and AI news, and organize seminars, workshops and talks for deeper exploration and exchange. Our aim is for high-school students to learn from one another and build solid, practical AI skills together.
